# For hosts: a calm page, complete drafts

## What changed
- `/provider` is one centred column with a title and two tabs, **List an experience** and **Who wanted it**, instead of two dense panels side by side against the page edges.
- Listing is two steps: *Describe it* (one text box), then *Check your listing* in six small labelled groups (Basics, Where, When, Price and group, What it's about, Good to know). Days are toggle chips; only the chosen tags show, with "Edit tags" for the full list; Cancel/Publish at the end. Publishing switches to *Who wanted it* for the new listing.
- *Who wanted it*: picker, three stat tiles, one line of counts, "Suits" segments, then short sections (why they didn't get you + tips, when they were free, budgets) and the actions in one row.
- **Bug fixes found while testing:**
  - The AI draft (free-tier NVIDIA model) often returns the schema's defaults (no title, ₹0, 10:00–18:00, every day). `provider.merge_draft` now lets the rule drafter's exact facts win (price, minutes, hours, days, group size, age, landmark) and fills anything the AI left blank; an invented landmark is dropped.
  - After publishing, insights showed the *previously* selected experience (stale state).
  - Other hosts' listing places ("Salim, near Tripolia Bazaar") were matched as landmarks for new drafts, leaving "bazaar" in the text and adding wrong shopping tags.
- Old side-by-side provider CSS removed.

## Why
The user: make the For Hosts page neat and uncluttered, like Explore.
