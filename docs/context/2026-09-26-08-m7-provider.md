# 2026-09-26-08 · M7: provider onboarding, availability, demand insights

## What changed
- `main` was fast-forwarded to M6 (`fda4ff8`) at the team's request.
- **`backend/app/store.py`** (new) uses stdlib `sqlite3`: the `listings`, `paused` and `demand` tables. `DB_PATH` sets the file, defaulting to `backend/data/local.db` (gitignored).
  - `current_seed(base)` overlays provider listings onto the read-only JSON seed. Paused experiences keep their data but get empty availability, so the engine needs no changes.
  - `log_demand` stores **aggregates only**: no location, no text.
- **`backend/app/provider.py`** (new):
  - **`ListingDraft`** is a flat, LLM-friendly listing.
  - **`draft_rules`** is the offline parser. It reads the name ("I'm Salim"), title ("Make your own bangle with Salim"), price and price model, duration, hours, closed days, minimum age, capacity, tags (plus provider keywords like bangle/lac, kids welcome, make your own), category, community-led, indoor/outdoor and accessibility. It uses the nearest landmark, and strips the place name first so "near Tripolia Bazaar" isn't read as a shopping tag.
  - **`draft()`** uses Claude through the shared `claude_parse`, with `llm_or_rules` falling back to the rules.
  - **`to_listing`** validates a reviewed draft at the trust boundary: known landmark, hours ≥ duration, at least one tag, at least one day. It then builds the Provider (informal), Place (pinned at the landmark) and Experience, with provider evidence dated today and `tourist_index` 0.1.
  - **`insights`** reports matching searches, times shown, why the experience wasn't chosen (bucketed into actionable reasons), tips, when those travelers were free, budget per person, how many had kids, and what else they wanted.
- **`backend/app/intent.py`** was refactored for reuse: `claude_parse` (a generic structured extraction), `llm_or_rules`, `known_places`, and the `money`, `clock_range` and `duration` helpers. The behaviour is unchanged; the tests prove it.
- **`backend/app/main.py`**:
  - Every endpoint now uses `seed()`, which is the base seed plus the overlay.
  - `/chat` logs demand.
  - `/catalog` now also returns `provider_listings`, `paused` and `vocabulary` (tags, categories, accessibility), so the UI never hard-codes them.
  - New endpoints: `POST /providers/draft`, `POST /providers/listings`, `POST /providers/availability`, `GET /providers/insights/{id}`.
- **Frontend:**
  - Traveler/Provider tabs in the header.
  - **`ProviderView.tsx`**: describe → "Draft my listing" → an editable form (landmark and category selects, numbers, time inputs, day checkboxes, tag toggle chips from the vocabulary, accessibility/indoor/weather/community checkboxes) → Publish.
  - The insights panel shows stats, "why interested travelers didn't get you" bars, tips, an hours histogram, budget bands, "also wanted", a Pause/Resume toggle, and a privacy note.
  - `api.ts` now shows pydantic 422 messages in readable form.
- **Tests:** 59 pass, 1 skipped.
  - `tests/conftest.py` gives every test its own temp DB.
  - `tests/test_provider.py` covers the rule draft, **scenario C end to end**, 422 validations, pause/resume, lost-demand insights ("over their budget" and the cheaper tip), and that the demand log has no location or text columns.

## Known gaps
- Anyone can publish or pause any experience, including seed ones. There's no provider auth (M9). That's fine for a local demo, but not for real use.
- A provider can't edit a listing after publishing; they'd delete the DB or add a new one. Add `PUT /providers/listings/{id}` if needed.
- Insights "matching" is tag overlap only. It doesn't weight by how close the search was in space or time.
- The Claude drafting path isn't run live (no credentials here). It shares the `claude_parse` call, whose request shape is tested offline.
- New listings are pinned exactly at their landmark's coordinates. A map-click location picker is M9.
