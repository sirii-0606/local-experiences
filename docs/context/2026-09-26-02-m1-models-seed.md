## What changed
- `backend/pyproject.toml` sets up the backend. The only runtime dependency is pydantic; pytest and ruff are dev extras.
- `backend/app/models.py` adds the Pydantic models for every entity in doc §4.1/§14:
  - Supply side: `Place`, `Provider`, `Experience`, `AvailabilityWindow`, and `Evidence`.
  - Traveler side: `Traveler` (the stable profile of one group member) and `TravelerState` (the situation right now).
  - Planning and signals: `Stop`/`Itinerary`, `ContextEvent`, and `Feedback`.

- `backend/tests/test_seed.py` has 4 tests: the seed loads, places are inside the Jaipur bounding box, every experience has at least one window it can fit in, and every provider offers something.

## Why
Every later milestone (engine, API, UI, provider side) needs a shared vocabulary and realistic data to test against.

## Key modelling choices (see `models.py` docstrings)
- **Group = list of `Traveler`s.** Group size is `len(group)`, the minimum age is the youngest member, and required accessibility is the union across members. This gives M8's fairness scoring (`0.7·mean + 0.3·min`) per-person interests to work with.
- **`AvailabilityWindow`:** the experience must start and finish inside `[start, end]`. If `slots` is set, it can only start at those times. If `on_date` is set, it's a one-off event (e.g. the JKK folk evenings on 2026-09-27 and 2026-10-03).
- **Confidence is not stored.** The engine will derive it from `Evidence` (source weight × recency decay, see `decisions.md`). Some items are deliberately stale or provider-only (`ex-sunrise-yoga` hours from 2025-11, `ex-puppet-making`, `ex-block-print-workshop`) so the low-confidence badge has something to show.
- **Seed honesty:** public monuments use their real names and approximate hours and prices. **Every business is fictional.**
- **Scenario C:** there are deliberately **no lac-bangle makers** in the seed. The provider-onboarding demo adds one live.
