## §16 open questions → prototype answers
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Minimum traveler state | location, available window (start/end), budget (₹ total), group (adults, children, seniors), intent tags. Everything else optional. |
| Hard vs soft constraints | **Hard (always):** fits time window incl. travel, open/available at that time, total price ≤ budget, group size ≤ capacity, required accessibility met, min age met, can still reach the end point (hotel/station/next stop) by the cutoff. **User-toggleable hard:** indoor-only, max distance. **Soft:** interests, crowd preference, localness, pace, outdoor preference. |
| Localness definition | `localness = 0.5·community_led + 0.3·(provider based in same neighbourhood) + 0.2·(1 − tourist_index)`, each 0..1 in seed. |
| Evidence for "verified/available/accessible" | Each attribute carries `{value, source: provider/traveler/verified, updated_at}`. Confidence = source weight (verified 1.0, traveler-reported 0.7, provider 0.6) × (0.5 + 0.5·0.5^(age/90 days)), so old info decays to half its weight but never to zero. With no evidence: 0.5 if the provider is verified, else 0.35, plus up to 0.25 for review volume (reviews/2000). A listing's confidence is the mean over hours and price, plus accessibility when the group needs it. *(Implemented in M2; 30-day half-life flagged nearly everything.)* |
| Uncertainty in ranking | Multiply quality term by confidence; show a "low confidence" badge below 0.5. **Never hide.** Exception: a *hard* accessibility requirement needs confidence ≥ 0.5 to pass. |
| Relevance vs novelty | `novelty_weight` slider (default 0.15); boosted when the user says "hidden / less touristy / local". Popularity is positive when they ask for "iconic / must-see". |
| Group disagreement | Hard constraints = union of everyone's. Soft score = `0.7·mean + 0.3·min` of members' fit (penalizes leaving one person miserable). |
| User-locked itinerary | Locked stops are never moved. If a change breaks one, it's reported as `at_risk` and left for the user. **Exception:** if the provider cancels or closes it, it's replaced, because it can no longer happen. Completed and in-progress stops are frozen. Only unlocked upcoming stops and gaps are replanned. |
| Explicit vs inferred feedback | Explicit: accept, reject (with reason chip), rating. Inferred: skip/abandon. Inferred signals get weight 0.3 of explicit. **M8 implementation** (`engine/learn.py`): accept is +0.3 per taste tag; "not my thing" is −0.3; no reason (skip, or reject without a reason) is −0.1; practical reasons (too expensive, too far, wrong time) change nothing. Suitability tags (`family`, `kids`, `vegetarian`, `evening`) are never learned. Learned values live in `TravelerState.learned` (−1..1). They're shown to the traveler, who can forget any of them, and they feed a `learned` ranking factor (weight 0.20) averaged over the experience's taste tags, so one shared generic tag isn't "similar". A reject also hides the item for the session (`rejected`, shown in "why not"). Providers see only aggregate accept/pass counts and pass reasons. Ratings after completion aren't built yet. |
| Provider structured vs free-form | Structured (required): title, category, duration, price model, location, hours/slots, capacity, accessibility flags. Free-form: description, story (LLM extracts tags from this). **M7 implementation:** free text becomes an editable `ListingDraft` (Claude, or offline rules), which the provider reviews and publishes. Publishing checks: a known nearest landmark (the listing is pinned there), hours at least as long as the experience, at least 1 tag and at least 1 day. New listings start with `tourist_index` 0.1 (not yet discovered) and provider-sourced evidence dated today, so they aren't flagged low confidence while the facts are fresh. |
| Provider availability & demand (M7) | **Pause:** the experience's availability is cleared, so discovery skips it and replans treat it as unavailable. **Demand log:** aggregates only (start hour, duration, budget, group size, kids, intents, shown ids, exclusion reasons). No location, no text. **Insights:** "matching" means a search whose intents share a tag with the experience. Exclusion reasons are bucketed into actionable categories (over budget, hours, too long, age, access, capacity, distance, weather), with a tip for the top ones. |
| Good alternative during replanning | Try the smallest change first: (1) **retime** the same experience later in the freed gap, then (2) **replace** it with the best-ranked item that shares at least one tag with the original, fits the gap (reachable from the previous stop and in time for the next) and fits the remaining budget, then (3) **drop** it. *(M4: the "+20% price" rule was dropped. The remaining budget already bounds it.)* |
| Replanning events (M4) | **Rain:** a hard constraint that excludes outdoor, weather-sensitive items. **Heat:** a soft penalty. **Delay:** you're free at max(now, end of the current stop) + delay. **Fatigue:** pace becomes relaxed (20 min slack), and strenuous items (active/adventure, or over 150 min) are replaced. **Budget change:** the amount is what's left for *unlocked* plans ahead; spending and locked bookings are kept. |
| Post-visit ratings (M9) | A 1–5 star rating plus "as described?" is independent traveler evidence (doc §6.3). The rating and review count are folded in. If most visits say it was as described, the latest visit date becomes `traveler` evidence for `availability` and `price_inr`, replacing provider claims and older traveler evidence but never `verified`. `ponytail:` disputes block the upgrade but don't downgrade existing evidence. There's no check that the rater actually visited; add booking-linked ratings before relying on it. |
| Provider ownership & bookings (M9) | Publishing returns a secret edit token, stored as a sha256 hash and compared in constant time. Edit, pause and delete of a provider listing need it. Curated seed experiences are open in the demo unless `SEED_ADMIN_TOKEN` is set. **Bookings** are a stub with no payment: they hold spots per (experience, start time) against capacity and confirm and lock the stop. `ponytail:` discovery doesn't yet subtract booked spots; `/bookings` refuses a full slot with a clear reason. |
| Where immersive adds value | Out of MVP. Roadmap: 360° previews for accessibility/atmosphere checks before booking. |

## Utility weights (live in `backend/app/engine/rank.py`)
preference 0.25 · intent 0.20 · spatial 0.10 · budget 0.10 · quality×confidence 0.15 · localness 0.10 · context 0.10, **plus** `state.novelty × novelty` (novelty = 1 − tourist_index, or tourist_index when "iconic" is an intent). Then an MMR diversity re-rank with λ = 0.8 (similarity = Jaccard over tags + category + provider). *λ was 0.7, but that pushed intent-less items above intent matches.*

Factor definitions: preference = group-fair tag overlap per member (capped at 3); intent = tag overlap (capped at 2); spatial = 1 − travel_min/60; budget = 1 − cost/budget; quality = Bayesian rating (prior 4.0 from 20 reviews) mapped 3–5 → 0–1, × confidence; context = mean(crowd fit, pace fit).
